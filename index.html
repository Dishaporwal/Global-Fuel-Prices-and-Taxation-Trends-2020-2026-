01 / Project overview

Fuel prices are more than
a number on a sign.

Pump prices can reflect global energy markets as well as national taxation, subsidies, exchange rates, and local policy. This project brings the views together to make comparisons easier to explore.

⌖

Compare across countries

Explore how fuel prices vary geographically and investigate which regions or countries stand out in the dashboard.

05 / How to use this project

From exploration to insight.

A simple workflow for exploring the dashboard responsibly and presenting evidence-based conclusions.

Explore

Start with the overview and identify the measures and dimensions shown in the dashboard.

Compare

Use available filters and chart selections to compare countries, regions, fuel types, or years.

Verify

Read labels, units, tooltips, and data definitions before drawing a conclusion from a visual pattern.

Communicate

Summarize the pattern, cite the relevant view, and distinguish correlation from causation.

Project takeaway

Look beyond the headline price.

Global fuel prices sit at the intersection of markets and policy. Use the dashboard and story together to examine the evidence, compare patterns, and form your own data-backed conclusions.
